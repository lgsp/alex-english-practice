Focus: Cyber-security, Vocabulary nuances & Grammar

1. Time Markers: For vs Since

FOR + Duration : "I have been working here for 2 years." (Depuis 2 ans - durée)

SINCE + Point in time : "I have been here since 2023." (Depuis 2023 - date précise)

2. Key Vocabulary

Evidence Material proof / Preuve matérielle.
Take-aways Key points to remember / Points clés.
Acknowledge To recognize / Reconnaître ou accuser réception.
In disguise Hidden / Déguisé.
Exchange Market place / Bourse d'échange.
Treason Betrayal / Trahison.

3. Common Confusion: Person, People & Peoples

  • One person: Une personne.
  • Two people: Deux personnes.
  • Two peoples: Deux peuples (ex: The French and the Germans).
