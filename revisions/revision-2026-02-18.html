⚠️ Common Confusions (Faux-amis)

  • To attend: Assister à (une réunion, un cours) / Not "aider".
  • To assist: Aider / Not "assister à".
  • Lawyer: Avocat / Liar: Menteur.
  • Dismissal: Licenciement / From the verb "to dismiss".
