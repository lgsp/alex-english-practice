2. Pronunciation Alert! ⚠️

Don't confuse these two words, they sound very different:

  • Sweet /swiːt/ : Douceur, sucré.
  • Sweat /swɛt/ : Transpirer.
    Example: "When I do sport, my sweatshirt becomes wet."
