Phonetic Battle: SH vs CH

During the session, we focused on the distinction between the "soft" sound and the "explosive" sound.

SH /ʃ/

Like Fish

Sounds like: "Chut!" (French)

CH /tʃ/

Like Chips

Sounds like: "Tche"

Vocabulary & Meaning

  • To stand for : Signifier (utilisé pour les acronymes).
    Example: "CIA stands for Central Intelligence Agency."
  • To conceive / To receive : Note the spelling (-ceive).
  • To hide : Cacher. (Pronunciation: /haɪd/)
  • Hidden : Caché (Participe passé / Adjectif).
  • Partnership : Une association (Association of several partners).
