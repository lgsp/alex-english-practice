Grammar Alert: The Greek Plural

Words of Greek origin like Crisis follow a specific pattern in English.

Singular: A crisis /ə ˈkraɪsɪs/

Plural: Crises /ˈkraɪsiːz/

Vocabulary Breakdown

  • Ominous : A bad sign (Inquiétant, de mauvais augure).
  • Useless : Without utility (Inutile).
  • Worthless : Without value (Sans valeur). Note: Something can be useful but worthless, or vice versa.
  • Bearable : Supportable. (From the verb to bear / supporter).
  • As usual : Comme d'habitude.
  • Business as usual : Les affaires continuent comme si de rien n'était (malgré une crise).
